Urban kit preview

Mock frames from the driver's chase view (camera 6.5 m behind the car, 2.4 m up, 342 km/h class speed). Drawn by a small script from the numbers in urban-spec.md: same sun (behind-left), same fog (#C9D6E2, 300 m to 1,600 m). Not a render of the game. Facade colours follow GDD 6.8 F8 (mid to dark, no flat white or light grey). The outer side (buildings, noise walls) is on the player's left, the median and the other carriageway on the right.

Facade palette

No bright white (every channel at most #BE), and no red or orange hues that could read like the red car. Muted, slightly warm or cool greys.

Facade tile array (8 slices, generated in code)

Each slice is 256 × 256 px: 2 bays × 2 floors, tiled by world metres. Alpha holds smoothness (walls 0.1, glass 0.6 to 0.8). Slice 7 is a flat roof and solid colour tile.
